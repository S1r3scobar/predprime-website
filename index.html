01 / The problem

A venue API is only the starting point.

Adding prediction markets means building more than a trading screen. Brokers need to connect market data, client pricing, order handling and risk controls into their existing operations.

01

Integration complexity

Venue-specific APIs, order formats and settlement mechanics create additional work for product and engineering teams.

02

Execution decisions

Order book depth, changing quotes and residual exposure need to be considered when handling client orders.

03

Broker requirements

Client pricing, position tracking and account-level controls need to work alongside the venue connection.

02 / Our solution

Connect your platform.
Build on our infrastructure.

PredPrime is B2B middleware between brokers and prediction market venues. Our goal is to reduce the infrastructure brokers need to develop and maintain themselves.

We are developing an MVP focused on Polymarket, with an adapter-based architecture designed for future venue expansion.

Connect

Bring market data and venue connectivity into a consistent integration layer.

Configure

Apply client-facing pricing and define how orders and exposure are handled.

Execute & monitor

Coordinate internal matching, external routing and position tracking through a shared workflow.

03 / Core capabilities

Designed around the broker workflow.

The MVP brings together the core building blocks of prediction market infrastructure. Production readiness and additional venue integrations remain part of our development roadmap.

MARKET DATA

Liquidity access

Market discovery and order book data, starting with Polymarket. Future aggregation is built around venue adapters.

PRICING

Configurable spreads

A pricing layer that separates venue prices from client-facing quotes and supports broker markup configuration.

EXECUTION

Order handling

A structured order lifecycle designed to coordinate validation, routing and execution status.

INTERNALIZATION

Matching & netting

Logic for matching opposing internal flow and identifying residual exposure for external hedging.

ACCOUNT CONTROLS

Positions & risk

Position tracking, average entry prices, PnL and margin calculations as inputs to broker risk workflows.

INTEGRATION

API-first architecture

A modular approach for connecting brokerage systems and adding venue-specific adapters over time.
